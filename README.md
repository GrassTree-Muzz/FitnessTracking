# Ol'Man Muz
how i track my health

Garmin activities (runs, paddles, tennis and the rest), sleep and heart rate come from Android Health Connect in the Android app, and they stay on the phone. See [the app README](html-to-app-android/README.md#garmin-data-health-connect). The earlier live cloud sync is archived in [archive/garmin-cloud-sync](archive/garmin-cloud-sync/README.md).

The Home page shows a 7-day and hourly forecast from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for your current location or a saved place, plus headline tiles for sleep, your last activity, steps and active days.
